# Equipment Request Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs the process for requesting, approving, and procuring equipment for employees of TechCorp Industries.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all full-time employees, contractors, and temporary staff who require equipment to perform their duties.

## 3. Equipment Categories

### 3.1 Standard Equipment
- Laptops and desktops
- Monitors and displays
- Keyboards, mice, and peripherals
- Headsets and webcams

### 3.2 Specialized Equipment
- Server hardware
- Networking equipment
- Development tools and licenses
- Testing devices

## 4. Approval Requirements

### 4.1 Cost Thresholds

| Cost Range | Approval Required |
|-----------|------------------|
| Below ₹10,000 | Team Lead approval |
| ₹10,000 - ₹25,000 | IT Department processing |
| ₹25,000 - ₹50,000 | Manager approval required |
| ₹50,000 - ₹1,00,000 | Director approval required |
| Above ₹1,00,000 | VP approval and procurement review |

### 4.2 Standard Processing
Equipment requests below ₹25,000 can be processed directly by the IT department after policy verification. No additional management approval is needed for standard equipment within this range.

### 4.3 Manager Approval
Equipment requests above ₹25,000 require explicit approval from the requesting employee's direct manager. The manager must verify:
- Business justification
- Budget availability
- Alignment with department goals

## 5. Request Process

1. Employee submits equipment request through the system
2. System verifies employee information
3. System checks equipment policy compliance
4. Based on cost threshold, request is either:
   - Routed to IT for direct processing (below ₹25,000)
   - Routed to manager for approval (above ₹25,000)
5. Upon approval, IT processes the procurement
6. Employee is notified of the decision

## 6. Budget Constraints

- Each department has an annual equipment budget
- Equipment requests cannot exceed the remaining departmental budget
- Emergency requests may bypass budget constraints with VP approval

## 7. Equipment Lifecycle

- Standard equipment refresh cycle: 3 years
- Employees must return equipment upon departure
- Damaged equipment must be reported within 48 hours
