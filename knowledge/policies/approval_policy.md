# Approval Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the approval framework for all business decisions and resource requests at TechCorp Industries.

## 2. Approval Authority Levels

### 2.1 Level 1 - Team Lead
- Budget authority: Up to ₹10,000
- Scope: Minor supplies, accessories, consumables
- Response time: 1 business day

### 2.2 Level 2 - Manager
- Budget authority: Up to ₹50,000
- Scope: Equipment, software licenses, training
- Response time: 2 business days

### 2.3 Level 3 - Director
- Budget authority: Up to ₹1,00,000
- Scope: Major purchases, vendor contracts, hiring
- Response time: 3 business days

### 2.4 Level 4 - VP/C-Level
- Budget authority: Unlimited
- Scope: Strategic investments, large contracts
- Response time: 5 business days

## 3. Approval Process

### 3.1 Standard Approvals
1. Requester submits request with business justification
2. System validates request against policy
3. Request is routed to appropriate approver based on cost and type
4. Approver reviews and decides (approve/reject/request modification)
5. Decision is recorded in the audit log
6. Requester is notified of the outcome

### 3.2 Multi-Level Approvals
For requests exceeding the approver's authority level:
- Request must be approved at each level sequentially
- Rejection at any level stops the process
- Each approval is independently recorded

### 3.3 Emergency Approvals
- Emergency requests can bypass normal approval chains
- Must be retroactively approved within 5 business days
- Must include written justification for emergency status
- All emergency approvals are flagged for audit review

## 4. Approval States

| State | Description |
|-------|------------|
| PENDING | Awaiting approver review |
| APPROVED | Request approved |
| REJECTED | Request denied |
| MODIFICATION_REQUESTED | Approver requests changes |
| ESCALATED | Sent to higher authority |
| EXPIRED | Approval window exceeded |

## 5. Delegation

- Approvers may delegate authority during planned absence
- Delegation must be documented in the system
- Delegated approvals carry the same audit requirements

## 6. Conflict of Interest

- Approvers cannot approve their own requests
- Requests involving related parties must be escalated
- Self-approval attempts are automatically flagged

## 7. Audit Requirements

- All approval decisions must be recorded with timestamp
- Approver identity must be captured
- Business justification must be preserved
- Modification history must be maintained
