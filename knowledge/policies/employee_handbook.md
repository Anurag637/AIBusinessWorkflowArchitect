# Employee Handbook - TechCorp Industries

## 1. Company Overview

TechCorp Industries is a technology solutions company committed to innovation and employee development. This handbook outlines key policies and procedures for all employees.

## 2. Employment Categories

### 2.1 Full-Time Employees
- Standard 40-hour work week
- Eligible for all benefits
- Equipment provided by company

### 2.2 Contractors
- Project-based engagement
- Limited benefits
- Equipment may be provided on case-by-case basis

### 2.3 Temporary Staff
- Fixed-term employment
- Basic benefits only
- Standard equipment provided

## 3. Equipment and Resources

### 3.1 Standard Equipment Provision
Every full-time employee is entitled to:
- One laptop or desktop computer
- One monitor
- Standard peripherals (keyboard, mouse)
- Required software licenses

### 3.2 Equipment Requests
Additional equipment may be requested through the equipment request system:
1. Submit request with business justification
2. Specify equipment details and estimated cost
3. Await approval based on company equipment policy
4. IT department processes approved requests

### 3.3 Equipment Responsibilities
- Employees are responsible for maintaining company equipment
- Report damage or loss immediately
- Return all equipment upon termination
- Personal use of company equipment must be minimal

## 4. Leave Policy

### 4.1 Annual Leave
- 24 days per year for full-time employees
- Must be approved by direct manager
- Minimum 2 weeks notice for planned leave

### 4.2 Sick Leave
- 12 days per year
- Medical certificate required for 3+ consecutive days

## 5. Expense Reimbursement

### 5.1 Eligible Expenses
- Travel for business purposes
- Client entertainment (pre-approved)
- Professional development
- Work-from-home setup (one-time, up to ₹15,000)

### 5.2 Reimbursement Process
1. Submit expense report within 30 days
2. Include receipts for all expenses
3. Manager approval required
4. Finance processes within 2 pay cycles

## 6. Code of Conduct

- Act with integrity and professionalism
- Respect colleagues and maintain inclusive environment
- Protect company confidential information
- Report any policy violations through appropriate channels
- Comply with all applicable laws and regulations

## 7. IT Security

### 7.1 Password Policy
- Minimum 12 characters
- Changed every 90 days
- No password sharing

### 7.2 Data Protection
- Confidential data must be encrypted
- No unauthorized external storage
- Report data breaches immediately
