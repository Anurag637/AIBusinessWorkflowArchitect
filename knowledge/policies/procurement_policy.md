# Procurement Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy governs the procurement of goods and services for TechCorp Industries, ensuring cost-effectiveness, transparency, and compliance.

## 2. Procurement Methods

### 2.1 Direct Purchase
- For items below ₹25,000
- Single vendor quote acceptable
- IT department can process directly

### 2.2 Competitive Quotation
- For items between ₹25,000 and ₹2,00,000
- Minimum 3 vendor quotes required
- Selection based on price, quality, and delivery time

### 2.3 Formal Tender
- For items above ₹2,00,000
- Published tender with evaluation criteria
- Procurement committee review required

## 3. Vendor Requirements

### 3.1 Approved Vendor List
- All vendors must be on the approved vendor list
- New vendors require compliance verification
- Vendor performance is reviewed annually

### 3.2 Vendor Evaluation Criteria
- Price competitiveness
- Product quality and warranty
- Delivery reliability
- After-sales support
- Financial stability

## 4. Purchase Order Process

1. Approved request received from approval system
2. Procurement team identifies suitable vendors
3. Quotes obtained based on procurement method
4. Best vendor selected
5. Purchase order generated
6. Delivery tracked
7. Receipt confirmed
8. Payment processed

## 5. Budget Control

- All purchases must be within approved budget
- Over-budget requests require additional approval
- Monthly procurement reports generated for management

## 6. Delivery and Receipt

- All deliveries must be inspected upon receipt
- Damaged goods must be reported within 24 hours
- Asset tagging required for items above ₹5,000
- Inventory updated within 2 business days

## 7. Payment Terms

- Standard payment: Net 30 days
- Early payment discount: 2% for payment within 10 days
- Late payment penalties apply per vendor agreement
